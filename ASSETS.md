# Asset provenance and licenses

## 3D models

All curated 3D subjects are **original procedural geometry** written for this project in `src/runtime/models.js`. They are generated at runtime from three.js primitives (extrusions, lathes, tori, icosahedra, point grids); nothing is downloaded from third parties.

License: **CC0-1.0** (public domain dedication). Generated websites may redistribute them freely.

## Fonts

Generated sites load curated families from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License) at runtime. Fonts are not bundled, and system fallbacks are used offline.

## Brand

The Centixio logo, mark and favicon (`public/brand/*`, `src/app/icon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-icon.png`) are original to this project.

## User uploads

Uploaded images and GLB/glTF models remain the user's property and are stored in a private bucket. They are included in exports only when the site references them.
